Metro areas: the 56 CBSAs of 1.0M+ residents (OMB July 2023 delineation, Vintage 2025 estimates). Districts (0.35–1.5M) are atlas units, that is county pieces and counties, of 0.35M+, with smaller counties joined by where their residents work. Subregions (0.6–3.5M, aiming at 1–2.5M) exist only in the 26 CBSAs of 2.5M+: OMB metropolitan divisions, with larger divisions and undivided CBSAs split by commuting. The district view is shown in subregions over 1.5M and in mid-size CBSAs with two or more districts; elsewhere it falls through to the subregion or CBSA. Work inside: employed residents whose job is in the same area (LEHD LODES 2024, Michigan 2021, cross-state commuting included). Spec: docs/specs/subregions-districts.md.

County pieces: Census 2020 tracts (cartographic boundaries) and Vintage 2025 county estimates; 2020 redistricting data (PL 94-171) for the population mix; LEHD LODES 2024 workplace and origin–destination files (Michigan 2021; in-state jobs only, so "work inside" excludes cross-state commuting). Official pieces follow each county's planning or statistical division; inferred pieces come from Louvain on commuting. Spec: docs/specs/county-pieces.md.
