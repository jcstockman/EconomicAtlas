Indicators: 46 per area from additive atlas-unit counts (rates are summed numerators over summed denominators; medians are estimated from summed brackets). Sources: Census 2020 and Vintage 2025; ACS 2020–2024 five-year, summed from tracts; BLS QCEW 2024; BEA 2024 (no Puerto Rico). Blank: withheld at the county by BLS or BEA, or not covered. US: the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico (BEA without Puerto Rico; QCEW from BLS's national total). Spec: docs/specs/atlas-indicators.md.

Metro areas: the 57 CBSAs of 1.0M+ residents (OMB July 2023 delineation, Vintage 2025 estimates), and the rest of Puerto Rico as one region (its other metros are all under 0.3M). Districts (0.35–1.5M) are atlas units, that is county pieces and counties, of 0.35M+, with smaller counties joined by where their residents work. Subregions (0.6–3.5M, aiming at 1–2.5M) exist only in the 26 CBSAs of 2.5M+: OMB metropolitan divisions, with larger divisions and undivided CBSAs split by commuting. The district view is shown in subregions over 1.5M and in mid-size CBSAs with two or more districts; elsewhere it falls through to the subregion or CBSA. Work inside: employed residents whose job is in the same area (LEHD LODES 2024, Michigan 2021, cross-state commuting included; Puerto Rico, which LODES does not cover: ACS 2016–2020 county-to-county flows). Spec: docs/specs/subregions-districts.md.

County pieces: Census 2020 tracts (cartographic boundaries) and Vintage 2025 county estimates; 2020 redistricting data (PL 94-171) for the population mix; LEHD LODES 2024 workplace and origin–destination files (Michigan 2021; in-state jobs only, so "work inside" excludes cross-state commuting). Official pieces follow each county's planning or statistical division; inferred pieces come from Louvain on commuting. Spec: docs/specs/county-pieces.md.
